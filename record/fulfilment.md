## Live state

- **`automation_response` defaults to `per_component`** *(gated)*. `uniform`
  stays reachable and reproduces every pre-Phase-2 figure exactly, so a published
  number can be REPRODUCED rather than merely disbelieved.
- **ε is two quantities.** As an INPUT, `machine_capability` says what machines
  can take; as an OBSERVABLE, `observable_epsilon()` is `machine_eoh /
  total_eoh`. Under `per_component` the observed share is strictly below the
  supplied index everywhere except zero — capability 0.99 gives an observed
  0.9085. `epsilon=` and `machine_capability=` are one quantity under two names;
  supplying both with different values raises.
- **The arc endpoint is derived, not conventional.** `observable_epsilon_ceiling()`
  = `1 − personal_share · Σ share_c · floor_c`, reading **exactly 1.000 under
  `uniform`** *(gated)* and below 1 under `per_component`. **ε does not reach 1
  because care resists automation.** The ceiling is an UPPER bound on itself —
  only two of four components carry a measured floor — and it depends on the
  obligation mix, so it is an empirical quantity rather than a constant.
- **`human_fraction` means the human share of the obligation** *(gated)*; the
  split factor `1 − ε` is reported separately as `uniform_split_factor`. They
  coincide exactly under `uniform`, and if they ever coincide again under
  `per_component` the floors have stopped biting.
- **A SUPPLIED STOCK IS NEVER RESCALED — capital, corpus or INHERITANCE** *(gated)*. ε FILLS an
  unspecified one along the canonical arc (`resolve_capital_stock`,
  `resolve_knowledge_base_size`, same priority as `monitoring_capability`); it
  never modifies one the caller supplied. Both are charged POST-growth, so the
  reported stock is the charged stock. Settles Block III's unfinished half and
  removes a latent stability condition.
  [capital-path-decision](#capital-path-decision),
  [knowledge-path-decision](#knowledge-path-decision),
  [trust-frame-closed](#trust-frame-closed).
- **Exactly ONE mint call site.** See
  [verification.md § Live state](verification.md#live-state).
- **`available_labor_eoh` defaults to unsupplied**, so the pipeline mints from
  obligation DEMANDED rather than SERVED. An institution that does not supply its
  labour data is measuring demand and calling it fulfilment. The implementation
  guide now says so at the intake table.
- **The partition closes to float equality** against `total_eoh()`: OBLIGATION
  (personal + civilisational knowledge), DELIVERY (infrastructure + apparatus
  knowledge), STOCK (thermal, restoration, visible deferred).
- **`fiscal_snapshot` takes `state=`** — nine quantities, not 19 loose
  keywords; supplying one both ways raises.

<a id="exit-rule-decided"></a>

**THE EXIT RULE IS THE PORTABLE ENDOWMENT, AND PRIOR WORK LIVES IN `savings`** (2026-09-17, author decision). No new machinery: `portable_endowment_individual` is already `P_ind = S + v(tenure)·D + savings`, and the third term was already additive and already excluded from the guarantee.

- **THE CHOICE, AND WHY IT WENT THIS WAY — MEASURED, NOT ARGUED.** A per-capita share of prior work claims the Trust to **exactly 100%** (it IS population × per-capita, by construction), leaving zero buffer; **127.4% once assets are included**, which is unfundable from a liquid balance because embodied capital cannot pay an exit without dismantling the apparatus. Sustainable turnover under the share rule is exactly the dividend rate, `DEP_RATE × DIV_RATE` = **1.8%/yr**; the portable endowment claims **18.6%** at ε=0 and sustains **~9.7%**. For scale, US interstate migration runs 2–3%/yr, so 1.8% would bind.
- **THE THREE TERMS NOW MEAN THREE THINGS.** `S` is the unconditional floor — membership-independent, never vests. `v(tenure)·D` is the share of what the collective accumulated, earned by being there. `savings` is what this member carried in, theirs and **at risk**, which is the correct standing for an asset claim.
- **AND THE ASYMMETRY IT LEAVES IS PINNED AS A FACT.** Full tenure buys **157.7 TEH** against the **8,760** a joiner may carry in — **1.8%, a factor of 55.6**. A lifetime of membership is worth one fifty-sixth of arriving with assets. **That is a `DEP_RATE`/`DIV_RATE` calibration question, not an exit-rule question**, and `TestPriorWorkEntersThroughSavings` fails if the ratio ever closes, because that would be a result worth noticing rather than absorbing.
- **A HYPOTHESIS OF MINE, REFUTED BY RUNNING IT.** I proposed that tenure substitutes for prior work, so a child born in accrues the full claim and the two-tier problem dissolves. Measured: native with full tenure and nothing brought reads χ **0.906** at ε=0 and **BREACHES**; joiner on day one carrying 8,760 reads **5.685** and clears. Tenure does not substitute. Second time this session a hypothesis of mine died on a cheap check, both times before publication.
- **AND THE COMPARISON WAS RUN ON A RETIRED INSTRUMENT.** χ = P/K_entry sets a one-year flow against a founding stock — the RC4 mismatch §8.9 retired. Which surfaces the real bind: **`exit_financing`, the ADOPTED invariant, takes no trust balance and no savings.** It cannot tell a member who brought 8,760 TEH from one who brought nothing, so NEITHER exit rule is expressible in the test that governs. Declared rather than built: adding a member-level resource term changes the adopted invariant and is an author decision under §3.
- **NOTHING DEDUCTS, EITHER.** `portable_endowment` has no caller that subtracts it from any balance — it is a reported per-capita diagnostic. `merge`/`split` already show the shape a real exit needs, asserting `teh_before == teh_after`. Until that path exists, both rules are descriptions rather than mechanisms.
- **THREE MORE BINDS RECORDED WHILE THEY ARE VISIBLE.** The care that raised a child **already minted, as wage to the carer**, so a child carrying a claim equal to it would count the hours twice. D5 moves a decedent's TEH **to** the Trust while an exit share would move it **out**, so dying and leaving would run opposite and a dying member would do better to emigrate first. And `population_growth_rate` is **net** — the model cannot presently tell a birth from an immigrant, which is the distinction the doctrine now rests on.

**THE INHERITANCE REPRICED TO 8,760 TEH/PERSON, AND RE-READ AS PRIOR WORK RATHER THAN AN ENTITLEMENT** (2026-09-17, author decision). Suite 4,840 passing (1 skipped), mypy clean on 102 files, provenance 346/346. Follows [trust-frame-closed](#trust-frame-closed), which made the inheritance travel with the population; this sets its level and says what it IS.

- **THE DOCTRINE.** What a person brings is PRIOR WORK — TEH, and assets that reduce EOH and carry EOH costs of their own — at FORMATION or on JOINING. 8,760 is what the current world's prior work is assumed to amount to per head: an estimate of a real stock, not a hard number and not a per-head grant. A subsistence founding brings 0.0 and a joiner may bring nothing. Leaving takes its share; a disbanded collective divides on population and federation rates, the contestability layer carrying value up to seed new collectives.
- **NOTHING IS MINTED BY ARRIVING, AND THAT IS WHAT MAKES IT IMPLEMENTABLE.** Prior work already exists, so bringing it is a TRANSFER. Failure mode 6, the eighth instance and the second with a gate behind it — the sibling of [capital-frame-closed](#capital-frame-closed), which closed the other constant named in that mode. - **WHAT WAS WRONG.** `TRUST_BASE_TEH` is declared `units: TEH (at the 1M reference population)` and was consumed as an absolute by every caller that moved the population. At the US frame the understatement is **335×**: 335M people inheriting the Trust of 1M, **104.48 TEH/capita against 35,000**. The repair is `core.fiscal.resolve_trust_balance(balance, population)` — unsupplied resolves along the frame, supplied is returned untouched.
- **THE INSTRUMENT WAS WRONG BEFORE THE FIX WAS.** The first scan keyed on the parameter NAME `trust_balance` and found 22 sites. Re-keyed on the QUANTITY — "what defaults to `TRUST_BASE_TEH`" — it found **four more the name filter structurally could not see**: `levy_schedule_for_chi(trust_start=)`, `contestability_audit(collective_trust=)` (same quantity, second and third names), `CollectiveFrame.trust_balance` (a dataclass FIELD, not a parameter), and `civilization_epsilon`'s `civ.get("trust_balance", TRUST_BASE_TEH)` (a dict-get default). **26 sites resolved, not 22.** This is failure mode 8 inside a mode 6 repair: the label names the department, the field names the quantity. The gate is keyed on the quantity for exactly this reason.
- **AND `contestability_audit` WAS MIS-CLASSIFIED AS FRAMELESS BY THE SAME ERROR ONE LEVEL DOWN** — the re-scan tested `'population' in names` as an EXACT match where the first used a substring, so a function carrying `collective_population` was filed as having no frame. - **THE CLI WAS THE WORST INSTANCE, AND IT WAS FOUND LAST.** **Ten** argparse definitions defaulted `--trust-balance` (and `--collective-trust`) to the constant while `--population` sat beside them freely settable, so `eoh dashboard --population 335000000` ran 335M people on a 1M-person Trust. Every fix above the CLI is invisible there. Found only because the advisor's checklist said to look; the first pass grepped `utils/eoh_cli.py` alone, found nothing, and recorded the item as closed — the CLI is split across `utils/*_cmd.py`. All ten now default `None`, following the `--capital-stock` precedent set by the 2026-09-09 capital-path decision four lines away in the same file.
- **IDEMPOTENCE IS WHAT MADE A UNIFORM REPAIR SAFE.** `resolve_trust_balance` returns a supplied value untouched, so resolving an already-resolved balance is a no-op. That removed the need to classify 26 sites by whether each FORWARDS the balance to a resolver or CONSUMES it arithmetically — and the classification would have been wrong: `make_federation` splits it (`trust_per = balance / n`) and `portable_endowment` computes a dividend from it, both of which fail on `None`.
