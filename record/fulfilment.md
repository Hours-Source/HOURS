# The EOH → TEH pipeline

**Scope.** The automation response (uniform vs per-component), machine capability
vs observable ε, the single mint path, the three obligation accounts, arc
stability, and the state container that feeds them.

## Live state

- **`automation_response` defaults to `per_component`** *(gated)*. `uniform`
  stays reachable and reproduces every pre-Phase-2 figure exactly, so a published
  number can be REPRODUCED rather than merely disbelieved.
- **ε is two quantities.** As an INPUT, `machine_capability` says what machines
  can take; as an OBSERVABLE, `observable_epsilon()` is `machine_eoh /
  total_eoh`. Under `per_component` the observed share is strictly below the
  supplied index everywhere except zero — capability 0.99 gives an observed
  0.9085. `epsilon=` and `machine_capability=` are one quantity under two names;
  supplying both with different values raises.
- **The arc endpoint is derived, not conventional.** `observable_epsilon_ceiling()`
  = `1 − personal_share · Σ share_c · floor_c`, reading **exactly 1.000 under
  `uniform`** *(gated)* and below 1 under `per_component`. **ε does not reach 1
  because care resists automation.** The ceiling is an UPPER bound on itself —
  only two of four components carry a measured floor — and it depends on the
  obligation mix, so it is an empirical quantity rather than a constant.
- **`human_fraction` means the human share of the obligation** *(gated)*; the
  split factor `1 − ε` is reported separately as `uniform_split_factor`. They
  coincide exactly under `uniform`, and if they ever coincide again under
  `per_component` the floors have stopped biting.
- **Exactly ONE mint call site.** See
  [verification.md § Live state](verification.md#live-state).
- **`available_labor_eoh` defaults to unsupplied**, so the pipeline mints from
  obligation DEMANDED rather than SERVED. An institution that does not supply its
  labour data is measuring demand and calling it fulfilment. The implementation
  guide now says so at the intake table.
- **The partition closes to float equality** against `total_eoh()`: OBLIGATION
  (personal + civilisational knowledge), DELIVERY (infrastructure + apparatus
  knowledge), STOCK (thermal, restoration, visible deferred).
- **`fiscal_snapshot` takes `state=`** — ten quantities instead of 19 loose
  keywords — and supplying a quantity both ways raises.

<a id="epsilon-split-capability-vs-observable"></a>

**ε SPLIT INTO CAPABILITY AND OBSERVABLE — THE PARAMETER WAS BEING QUOTED AS THE OUTCOME, AND THE ARC ENDPOINT IS NOW DERIVED** (2026-09-01, author decision). `observable_epsilon()`, `observable_epsilon_ceiling()`, `machine_capability=` on `human_eoh_per_domain` and `eoh_to_teh_pipeline`, both quantities reported. 39 tests, 3,689 pass, mypy clean on 88 files. **No shipped number moves — `epsilon=` is unchanged and every existing caller behaves identically.**
- **PHASE 2 SPLIT THEM THE DAY BEFORE AND NOBODY LOOKED.** ε has been doing two jobs: as an INPUT it says what machines are capable of taking; as an OBSERVABLE it is DEFINED as `machine_eoh / total_eoh`. Under `uniform` the split is exactly (1−ε) on every domain, so the share recovered from the split IS the number fed in and the distinction is invisible. Under `per_component` the personal domain keeps a floored human share, so **the observed share is strictly below the supplied index everywhere except zero**: capability 0.40 → observed 0.365, 0.90 → 0.825, **0.99 → 0.9085, a gap of 0.081**.
- **IT WAS ABOUT TO BE PUBLISHED AS THEORY.** The value-anchor draft said ε *is* "the share of the obligation still dependent on human agency". At an index of 0.99 the parameter says 1% and the ledger says ~9.15%. **The section would have stated a claim the code stopped satisfying the day before it was written** — the reported-vs-applied defect one layer up, in prose rather than in a return dict. Caught by asking what §4 of the draft rests on, which is what that section is for.
- **THE ARC ENDPOINT IS NO LONGER A CONVENTION.** `observable_epsilon_ceiling()` = `1 − personal_share · Σ share_c · floor_c` and reads **0.912–0.918** depending on the obligation mix, against **exactly 1.000** under `uniform`. **ε does not reach 1 BECAUSE care resists automation**, derived rather than asserted — the author's reading (ε as human capacity *not required*, 0 = all required, and that is why it never closes) turned out to be the correct one and the code did not implement it.
- **THE CEILING IS AN UPPER BOUND ON ITSELF.** Only care carries a measured floor; nutrition, shelter and health carry none, which is an admission and not a zero. A test adds a synthetic health floor and asserts the ceiling FALLS, so the direction of the remaining error is pinned rather than described.
- **AND IT DEPENDS ON THE OBLIGATION MIX, NOT ONLY ON CAPABILITY.** A care-heavier economy has a lower ceiling — pinned, because it makes the ceiling an empirical quantity rather than a constant, and it is why no single number is quoted in the draft.
- **THE OBSERVABLE IS CAPTURED BEFORE THE LABOUR CONSTRAINT, AND THAT IS THE SUBTLE ONE.** A labour shortfall is DEFERRED, not machine-fulfilled, so computing the share after rationing would credit machines with obligation that simply went unserved. Moving the capture after the constraint fails **exactly one test** — the reasoning is pinned by the assertion that rationing does not move the machine share.
- **`epsilon=` AND `machine_capability=` ARE ONE QUANTITY UNDER TWO NAMES, AND SUPPLYING BOTH WITH DIFFERENT VALUES RAISES** — the `total_eoh` base-vs-area precedent. Supplying both with the SAME value is allowed: a caller migrating names should not be punished for being explicit. A full symbol rename across the suite is deliberately NOT bundled.
- **THE WIRING GATE HAD NEVER SEEN THE DOCUMENTED ENTRY POINT, AND THE FIX EXPOSED IT BY ACCIDENT.** The probe only reaches functions callable with no arguments; `eoh_to_teh_pipeline` required `epsilon`, so **the one function the implementation guide tells an institution to run was skipped entirely**. Giving that parameter a default made it visible and the gate immediately reported the two ecological intake fields as inert there. **Nothing became inert — the gate started looking somewhere it had never looked.** Ratchet 10 → 12 on pure coverage, with the reason written into the test because no counter can tell an honest rise from a fresh copy.
- **THREE MUTATIONS RUN AND ALL THREE BITE**: returning the capability from `observable_epsilon` fails 15; capturing after the constraint fails 1; making the alias prefer silently instead of raising fails 1. Restoration verified by re-running rather than by inspecting the file.

- **THE RESULT SUPPORTS THE FRAMEWORK'S CENTRAL CLAIM: the stationary band is CONTIGUOUS (ε ∈ [0.171, 0.990] as first reported at an UNDECLARED standard; superseded — see the entry above).** The system does not require growth to remain stable — it can stand still almost anywhere on the arc. **ε=0 is the exception and it fails on the OBLIGATION, not on the apparatus** (`delivery_pays` is True at subsistence), which is the repo's own recorded position — *subsistence can survive but cannot reach sufficiency without automation* — arriving from the stability side.
- **A SHAPE I DID NOT PREDICT: the HUMAN delivery cost peaks in the INTERIOR** (188.9 h/person·yr at ε=0.70, falling to 14.4 at ε=0.99), because delivery grows with the capital stock while (1−ε) collapses faster. **An arc test assuming monotonicity here would fail on a correct implementation** — the Ψ-bell trap in a new place, now pinned as its own fact.
- **TWO OF FIVE MUTATIONS DID NOT BITE ON THE FIRST PASS, AND BOTH WERE KNOWN PATTERNS.** Hard-coding `uniform_reproduces_shipped = True` passed everything, because the test verified the FACT independently and never the FLAG — the `psi`-vs-`psi_applied` reported-vs-applied shape. And hard-coding `contiguous = True` passed, because the real band IS contiguous, so no test could tell *checked* from *hard-coded* — a threshold that cannot fire, after `settlement_report`'s unconditional breach. **Fixed by making both falsifiable**: the anchor reference is injectable purely so the flag can report False, and the band logic is extracted as `band_from_flags()` so the non-contiguous branch is reachable on a synthetic input. Both now bite.

<a id="phase-0-obligation-accounts"></a>

**PHASE 0 OF THE ONE-OBLIGATION REFRAME — THE THREE ACCOUNTS, AND TWO DEFECTS THE FOUR-WAY SUM WAS HIDING** (2026-08-30). `scenarios/obligation_accounts.py` + `eoh scenario run obligation_accounts`, 26 tests, 3,503 pass, mypy clean on 85 files, provenance **290/290**, shadow held at 33. **REPORTING ONLY — `TestAccountsChangeNothing` fails the moment that stops being true.**
- **THE FOUR DOMAINS ARE NOT THE SAME KIND OF QUANTITY, AND SUMMING THEM HIDES THE DIFFERENCE.** **OBLIGATION** is what is owed independent of how it is met (personal + civilisational knowledge — neither exists because we built something). **DELIVERY** is what meeting it COSTS (infrastructure + apparatus knowledge — both exist ONLY because the apparatus does, and both are incurred to REDUCE the obligation). **STOCK** is what is owed from the past (thermal, restoration, visible deferred). The partition closes to float equality against `total_eoh` and is bound to its own keys on both sides, so it is a regrouping and not a recomputation.
- **THE HEADLINE: OBLIGATION IS NEARLY FLAT AND DELIVERY IS NOT.** Across the arc obligation moves **1,315.6M → 1,439.9M EOH (+9.4%)** while delivery moves **75.0M → 1,444.1M (+1,825%)**. Delivery/obligation runs **0.0570 → 1.0029, crossing 1.0 at ε≈0.989** — the apparatus costs more entropy than the obligation it serves. **Essentially ALL growth in total EOH is delivery cost**, and a four-way sum hides that by adding it to the obligation. The crossover is explicitly **NOT** reported as a failure: whether it is acceptable depends on how much the apparatus ABATES (Block II), which this account does not carry.
- **THIS IS NOT A RENAME OF BLOCK III's `basis="final"`, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS A DEFECT.** `total_base` puts the ecological STOCKS in the base — supply `thermal_obligation=1e8` and `total_base` rises by the full 1e8, so **legacy damage reads as though the system owed more going FORWARD**. The obligation account does not move. Pinned both ways.
- **AND IT FOUND THE CARE CONTRADICTION WITHOUT LOOKING FOR IT — THREE STATEMENTS, TWO AGREE.** `CARE_AUTOMATION_FLOOR`'s own tag block says relational care *"cannot be automated at any ε — a commitment about what care IS"*, and Block II reaches the same conclusion independently (care is the least abatable component, **84.4% of the residual**). But **`human_eoh_per_domain` applies the same (1−ε) to every domain**, and care is **62.1% of personal EOH** by the basket's own shares. At ε=0.99 the uniform human fraction is **0.0100** while the fiscal floor gives care **0.1585**; weighted, the implied personal human fraction is **0.1022 — 10.2× the uniform figure.** **The fiscal layer models care as un-automatable while the generation layer automates it at the full rate**: two accounts of one quantity, the `psi`-vs-`psi_applied` shape, and nothing had compared them.
