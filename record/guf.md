# The Ground Use Fee

**Scope.** Ψ and its retirement, the term basis (U / E / I), the ten `GUF_USE_*`
ratios and the ×100, the per-parcel term, the servicing and parcel censuses, the
conservation credit, NLSA boundary conditions.

## Live state

- **The fee is** `GUF(p) = max(floor, [Ψ_b·base_fee + Ψ_e·E + Ψ_i·I]·Ω + P(ε))`
  with `P(ε) = 4.723·α(ε)` TEH/parcel·yr
  (`GUF_PARCEL_RATE_TEH_PER_PARCEL_YR`, bound by test, not expression).
  P carries α and never Ψ, sits **outside** Ω, and is gated on
  `location_value > 0`.
- **`psi_policy` defaults to `retired`** (Ψ ≡ 1). `bell` and `flow_only` stay
  reachable and every NLSA §4.4 boundary condition is still pinned against
  `bell`. Ψ was retired for duplicating α at the high end and for a category
  error at the low end — see [term-basis-audit](#term-basis-audit-psi-retired).
- **`GUF_USE_SCALE_FACTOR` is still 100.0 and the ten ratios are untouched.**
  Phase 2 measured how far the fee sits from a servicing census and deliberately
  did not move it: a census settles a LEVEL and the defect is structural.
- **The conservation credit is clamped** (author sign-off, 2026-08-30): a credit
  may reduce a fee to zero, it may not pay out. The coefficient is live above
  that bound and must not be retired on this decision.
- **A parcel at L=0 pays exactly 0.0 at every ε** *(gated)*, with
  `floor_applied=False` — zero by ASSET ABSENCE. A conservation parcel on
  valuable ground has `guf_formula < 0` and `guf_applied == 0.0` with
  `floor_applied=True` *(gated)* — that is SUPPRESSION. The two must not be
  conflated.
- **GUF revenue is its own line in `fiscal_snapshot`, never folded into the
  levy** *(gated)*, and is circulatory — it moves the Trust, not `teh_created`.
  Derived one layer up in `scenarios/collective.collective_snapshot()`, because
  giving the fiscal layer a parcel inventory would assert that land tenure is
  physics.
- **E defaults to 0 on every shipped path** — reachable, not active. The package
  ships no measured per-hectare service profile.

## Open

- **The ten `GUF_USE_*` ratios.** Both censuses aggregate over LAND CLASSES while
  the fee table is indexed by USE CATEGORY. *Settles by:* occupational data coded
  by the land use it serves, or a change to the fee's definition. Declared in
  `tests/test_claims_register.py::DECLARED_OPEN`, so this one cannot go stale
  silently. See [guf-magnitude-options-1-2](#guf-magnitude-options-1-2) and
  [ten-ratios-decomposed](#ten-ratios-decomposed).
- **`P_service` — the service-point denominator.** `numunits` is contaminated by
  other columns pasted in county by county; mechanism-based exclusion cleans the
  tail but not the numerator, and the gap cannot be imputed. *Settles by:* Census
  housing-unit estimates by county (residential share only; commercial service
  points remain a separate gap).
- **`u_parcel`'s `resolves_by` is half satisfied.** The parcel count exists; the
  restriction to `SERVICED_LAND_CLASSES` does not, so the rate is a LOWER bound.
  *Settles by:* the `usedesc` normalisation project.

Adopted LIVE at the measured rate rather than form-only at 0.0 — my recommendation was form-only; **the author chose live.**
- **THE MOTIVATION WAS THE TEH CYCLE, AND THE MEASUREMENT DOES NOT SUPPORT IT.** The reasoning was that TEH minted with no recurring destination does not complete the cycle. Two corrections, both measured: **GUF is CIRCULATORY, not a drain** — revenue flows to the Trust and back out through the dividend, so it moves TEH rather than destroying it — and **the magnitude does not carry the argument**: on a frame of 10,000 urban parcels against 25,000 people the term is **0.03–0.34% of TEH created** across the arc and the whole of GUF is **0.25–2.9%**, i.e. ~1.9 TEH/person·yr against personal EOH of ~1,475. **The cycle concern stands and this is not the lever that answers it** — that question lives in domain balance. **The term was adopted on the SERVICING MEASUREMENT, which survives the magnitude finding intact.**
- **`subdivision_invariance` INVERTED, AND BOTH READINGS ARE PINNED.** It existed to FALSIFY Phase 2's density mechanism for the urban overshoot by returning **exactly** 1,151.34 h/ha·yr after splitting every parcel; it is now the REGRESSION ANCHOR for the term that fixed it, returning **×1.1194**. `ratio_area_only` recomputes at `parcel_rate=0.0` and must still be exactly 1.0 — **keeping the original falsification alive as a property of the area product rather than deleting the evidence for it**, so Phase 2's mechanism stays refuted.
- **THREE IMPLEMENTATION DECISIONS, EACH FOUND BY A TEST FAILING RATHER THAN REASONED TO IN ADVANCE.** (1) **P carries α and NEVER Ψ** — Ψ was retired for duplicating α, and applying it here would repeat exactly the defect that retired it. (2) **P sits OUTSIDE Ω** — Ω is the fraction of the ASSESSED QUANTITY in use, and a half-occupied parcel is still one parcel; its deed and assessment do not halve. (3) **P is GATED on `location_value > 0`, which preserves NLSA §4.4.** Ungated, a parcel at L=0 pays the term and the remote-end zero breaks — and that is **`P_title`/`P_service` arriving as a STRUCTURAL consequence**: title cost exists for any deed anywhere, refuse and metering need the parcel serviced, and this data cannot separate them. **The discontinuity at L→0 is real and is NOT smoothed**, because smoothing it would invent a ratio nothing measures.
- **THE FRAGMENTATION PREMIUM IS ε-INVARIANT, WHICH I DID NOT PREDICT.** ×1.1194 at ε = 0, 0.40 and 0.99 alike, because both P and the area product carry α(ε) and it cancels. The fee prices fragmentation by the same factor at subsistence and at post-scarcity.
- **AND THERE IS NOW A MINIMUM VIABLE CONSERVATION PARCEL.** The credit scales with AREA and P is FLAT, so below ~1 SLU at L=0.75 a conservation parcel pays a positive fee instead of reaching zero. **Found by the claims-register predicate failing at 1 SLU** — a 100 m² "conservation parcel" was the artifact, and at 0.1 ha and above the clamp still applies. Coherent with the decision (a sliver still needs a deed) and pinned as a behaviour change, with the `parcel_rate=0.0` control proving it is the term's doing and not the coefficient's.
- **`GUF_PARCEL_RATE_TEH_PER_PARCEL_YR` IS BOUND BY TEST, NOT BY EXPRESSION** — data.py sits below `reference/` and `scenarios/`, the `MEAN_MULTIPLIER_REFERENCE` precedent — and is pinned as the fee's actual DEFAULT, because **a constant declared and not wired is the stranded-parameter failure this repo has found four times.**
- **THE NLSA WORKED EXAMPLES ARE PINNED AT `parcel_rate=0.0`, NOT REWRITTEN.** NLSA Eq. 1 IS the pre-2026-08-30 fee; asserting the published example against a changed equation would silently rewrite the source.
