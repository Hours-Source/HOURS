**THERMAL MERGED INTO SHELTER, AND THE BASE DECLARES ITS CLIMATE** (2026-09-04, author decision, `5b4e842`). Basket 8 → 7 components; `PERSONAL_EOH_BASE_CLIMATE_FRAME` added. 4,071 pass, mypy clean on 90 files, provenance 314/314. Coverage unmoved at 6.9% — the priced component is not in the shelter bucket.
- **DEGREE-DAYS WAS NEVER A PER-PERSON QUANTITY.** The basket's form is Σ quantity_per_person_year × hours_per_unit and exactly one row violated it: a village of 100 and a city of a million in one climate both face 2,500 degree-days. `CLIMATE_CONDITIONING` had already recorded the anomaly by marking thermal `quantity_is_climate` while every other component was `delivery` — the classification named the defect and nothing acted on it.
- **THERMAL HAS NO QUANTITY OF ITS OWN; IT BORROWS SHELTER'S.** m² × degree-days × hours per (m²·degree-day) at a stated envelope. Degree-days is now an INTENSITY carried on the shelter row as `degree_days_per_year`, not a quantity. Climate now enters through exactly ONE channel — delivery — for every component, which is what lets the basket say *global quantities, local productivities*.
